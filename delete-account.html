Delete your account

CertRaid · Presto Enterprises LLC

You can delete your CertRaid account and the personal information tied to it at any time, using either option below.

Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings (see Help).

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open CertRaid and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

Option 2: By email

Email support@prestoenterprise.com from the address on your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We verify the request, delete the account within 7 days, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details, email address and profile (display name, avatar, exam date)
  • Your study history: lessons, quizzes, boss battles, flashcards, streaks, progress and bits
  • Your friends, league results and challenge results
  • Feedback you sent

What may be kept

  • Question reports you sent, no longer linked to you
  • De-identified or aggregated data that can't reasonably identify you
  • Data in routine backups and diagnostic logs, deleted on a rolling basis, generally within 30 days
  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google under their own policies, and anything we must keep by law

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
