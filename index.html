Subscriptions and the free trial

How does the free trial work?

The 3-month Exam Pass starts with a 3-day free trial. Everything unlocks right away. We remind you the day before the trial ends, and you're charged on day 3 unless you cancel first. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside CertRaid. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CertRaid → Cancel. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CertRaid → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid through. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play's refund request page. If something went wrong on our side, email us and we'll help.

I bought a subscription on another phone

Sign in with the same account and tap Restore on the subscription screen. A subscription bought through Apple can't move to Android or the other way around.

How does the Pass Guarantee work?

If you have an active 3-month Exam Pass, finish your full plan on the Path, score 80% or higher on at least two full practice exams, and still don't pass the exam, we give you 3 more months free. Email us your failing score report within 30 days of your exam date. The guarantee is extra time, not a cash refund. Full terms are in our Terms of Use.

Using the app

What counts toward my streak?

Finish a Path step, a quiz or Brute Force, or rate 10 flashcards. A streak freeze from the shop covers a missed day automatically.

How do leagues work?

Each week you compete in a league of up to 30 players at your tier, from Guest up to Root. The top 20% move up a tier when the week ends, and the bottom 20% of larger leagues move down. The top three earn bits. You only appear on leaderboards if you turn them on in Settings → Privacy.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password". We'll email you a one-time code to type into the app, then you can choose a new password.

Something in a question looks wrong

Tap "Report a problem" on the question, or send feedback from Settings → Help. We review every report.

Someone has an offensive name

Tap their name on the leaderboard and choose Report or Block. Blocked players disappear from your boards and aren't told. Manage blocks in Settings → Privacy.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, see delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your store settings first.
